# Posture: OPEN to AI crawlers, deliberately. TokenHUD's buyers ask assistants
# "how do I see what my coding agents are costing" — being citable is the
# distribution channel, so blocking the crawlers that answer that question
# would be blocking the customer. A product with nothing to hide on a
# marketing site loses nothing by being read.
